How ranking works

1. Add images

Choose photos from your computer. They land in the tray under the board.

2. Drag to lock

Drop an image on S through F. It stays in that row until you move it again.

3. Re-rank anytime

Drag between tiers, double-click to return to the tray, or reset the whole board.
