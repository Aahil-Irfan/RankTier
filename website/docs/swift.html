For Apple

One SwiftUI project for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Build it locally with Xcode — a compiled App Store IPA is not included.

  1. Download TierList-For-Apple-1.0.0.zip.
  2. Unzip and open TierListApp.xcodeproj in Xcode 15 or later.
  3. Choose an iPhone, iPad, or My Mac destination and press Run.
  4. Add images, then press-and-hold to drag them into a tier. On iPhone the rows stay compact so the board fits a phone screen.

Targets are iOS 17 and macOS 14. Set your Development Team under Signing & Capabilities to run on a physical iPhone or iPad.
